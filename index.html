Isaac Sun

isaacsun {at} cmu {dot} edu

Hi, I’m Isaac Sun, a sophomore at Carnegie Mellon University majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science. I’m broadly interested in building reliable and intelligent AI systems, from improving coding agents through pretraining to grounding vision-language models in robotic perception and action. I am currently looking for a long-term research group at CMU!

Last summer, I worked on RSI (Recursive Self-Improvement) at Stanford Pervasive Parallelism Lab with Qizheng Zhang and Professor Kunle Olukotun, where I co-developed Stateless Language Agents, exploring how AI agents can sustain research progress over long runs. The approach lets agents build on past experimental results while starting each new task with fresh, focused context.

Before CMU, I graduated from Lynbrook High School, where I competed in math olympiads (USAMO) and was part of the Lynbrook Math Team, which placed first at the Stanford Math Tournament in 2024 and second in 2025.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing basketball, hiking, and discovering new music. I’m also a huge fan of social deduction games (try Blood on the Clocktower) and solving puzzles like Scurry and LinkedIn games.
