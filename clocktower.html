QUEST LOG · ONE NIGHT IN TOWN
  1. Draw a role
    Everyone gets a secret identity. Most are good. A few are evil, and they know each other.
  2. Night falls
    Eyes closed. The Storyteller wakes roles one by one to act in secret: a poisoning, a vision, a kill.
  3. Day breaks
    Claims, counter-claims and whispered deals in the corner. Someone always asks to be trusted.
  4. The vote
    Nominate, argue, raise hands. Execute someone and hope it was the right call. Repeat until one side wins.

The Workshop

We port these games online

Club members design and build browser versions of the games we play, so friends can play from different dorms, cities or time zones. Our first is Clocktower Town, a gothic pixel-art town for private games of social deduction.

  • ◆Proximity voice. Talk to whoever stands near you; walk away to whisper.
  • ◆Storyteller tools. Run the night without a box of tokens.
  • ◆Point and pin. Mark who you suspect for the whole town to see.
  • ◆5 to 15 players. Private rooms with an invite link.
